Inferred atrophy risk level

    Inference, not diagnosis. The ground-based physician interprets and diagnoses.

    Details

    How it is calculated. The overall risk takes the higher level between squat load (ARED) and grip strength. Body mass is supporting data and sEMG is recorded only: neither changes the level. Symptoms from the last 7 days (a drastic change, or average fatigue or weakness of 7 or more) trigger a separate alert for the physician.

    Team thresholds based on published studies, not yet clinically validated. Strength and load: low < 10%, moderate 10–19.9% (according to NASA, leg strength drops by about 10–15% on missions of about 6 months), high ≥ 20% (high-risk threshold from Scott et al., 2023). Body mass: low < 2%, moderate 2–5% (first-month loss according to NASA), high > 5%.
